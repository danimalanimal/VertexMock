VERTEX Vertical Jump

Detection mode

Jump type

Counter-movement jump — squat then jump in one fluid motion. Tests reactive strength.

Attempts per athlete

Best of 3 is the standard. 30-60s rest between attempts.

How to set up

  1. 1
    Floor surface

    Hardwood, concrete or sprung gym floor. Not carpet, rubber mats, or grass — audio detection needs a hard surface to transmit impact sound.

  2. 2
    Phone placement

    Phone flat on the floor, 1-2m from the athlete's jump spot. Not under feet, not propped up. Volume up so cues are audible.

  3. 3
    Calibrate

    App listens for 2 seconds to learn the room's background noise. You'll see a verdict — quiet, mixed, or too loud.

  4. 4
    Verify with one jump

    Coach does a verification jump first. App detects take-off + landing and shows the waveform. Confirms everything works before any athlete is tested.

  5. 5
    Run the class

    Phone listens continuously. Athlete jumps → result appears. Tap ✓ to accept, ↺ to redo, or ✎ to override. Auto-advances after 4s.

Safety
  • 10-min warm-up: jog + dynamic squats + 2 practice half-effort jumps
  • Stop on knee or ankle pain — refer for assessment
  • Hard landings: cue athletes to bend knees on impact
