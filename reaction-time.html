VERTEX Reaction Time

Trials per athlete

5 trials — drops best + worst, averages the middle. Tests neural processing speed.

How to run reaction time

  1. 1
    Position

    Athlete seated, phone flat on the table or held in their dominant hand. Quiet environment, no distractions.

  2. 2
    Brief them

    "Wait for the screen to turn GREEN, then tap as fast as you can. Do not anticipate — tapping early voids the trial."

  3. 3
    Start

    Tap START → screen goes RED. Random delay 2-5s before turning GREEN. Athlete taps anywhere. Repeat across 5 trials.

  4. 4
    Early-tap penalty

    If athlete taps during the RED phase, trial counts as failed and a new random delay starts. No skipping.

  5. 5
    Score

    Reported in milliseconds. Drop best + worst, average middle trials. Typical adult: 200-250ms. Elite athletes: 150-200ms.

Tips
  • Same hand each trial — alternating dominant/non-dominant skews results
  • 30-60s rest between trials prevents anticipation patterns
  • Test at similar time of day for fair longitudinal comparisons
