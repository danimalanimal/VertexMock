How to run a time trial

  1. 1
    Course

    Confirm exact distance. On a 400m track: 1 lap = 400m, 4 laps = 1 mile, 12.5 laps = 5km (start at the staggered 5km line). For road, mark start + finish with cones.

  2. 2
    Brief athletes

    "Run as fast as you can sustain for the whole distance — even pacing wins, don't sprint the first lap." Encourage but don't pace them.

  3. 3
    Start cue

    "On your marks — set — GO." Audio beep on GO starts the clock. Stand at the finish line with your phone.

  4. 4
    Finish taps

    As each athlete crosses, tap their tile — their split locks immediately. Audio cue confirms. Undo gives you 5s if you mis-tap.

  5. 5
    Scoring

    Results ranked fastest first, with pace per km. Save to fitness stats so it appears on athlete progress reports.

Safety
  • 10-min jog + dynamic warm-up first
  • Hot day? Earlier start, water at start + finish
  • Cool-down jog 5 min then walk — never stop dead at the finish line
