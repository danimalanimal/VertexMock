Audio Clip Recorder

Help us train the rip/thud detector. Capture short labelled clips from real athletes; we'll use them to tune thresholds and (later) train a classifier.

How to record a clip

  1. Place the phone on the floor, 1–2 metres from where the athlete will land. Floor-coupled mic gives us the cleanest landing thud.
  2. For CMJ clips: apply packing tape across the ball of each shoe (half on shoe, half on floor) before the rep.
  3. Pick the matching label below. Be honest — mis-labelled data is worse than no data.
  4. Tap RECORD, then perform the action within the 4-second window. The clip auto-stops.
  5. Review the waveform, then tap UPLOAD. Repeat with a fresh strip of tape.

Clips upload anonymously to a shared library. No PII, no identifiers — just the audio waveform and the label you pick.
