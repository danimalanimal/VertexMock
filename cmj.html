VERTEX CMJ (Tape)

Detection mode

The tape trick

CMJ take-off is silent — feet leave the floor with no impact noise. To time flight from audio we use a single strip of packing tape across the ball of each foot: half on the shoe, half on the floor. When the athlete jumps, the tape rips free — that sharp broadband kkrrt is the take-off timestamp. Landing thud closes the window. Flight time = rip → land.

Replace the tape every rep. ~36 strips for a 12-athlete × 3-attempt session — bring a fresh roll.

Attempts per athlete

Best of 3 is the standard. 30–60s rest between attempts (CMJ recovers fast — it's a single concentric effort).

How to set up

  1. 1
    Floor surface

    Hardwood, concrete or sprung gym floor. Not carpet, rubber mats, or grass — both the tape rip and the landing thud need a hard surface to ring.

  2. 2
    Phone placement

    Phone flat on the floor, 1–2 m from where the athlete will stand. Not under feet, not propped up. Volume up so cues are audible.

  3. 3
    Apply the tape

    One 5 cm strip of clear packing tape per athlete per rep. Place it across the ball of the dominant foot: ~half on the front of the shoe, ~half stuck to the floor in front. The tape should be taut but not stretched. Apply fresh tape every attempt — once it rips, the adhesive is spent.

  4. 4
    Calibrate

    App listens for 2 seconds to learn the room's background noise. Threshold is set low — the tape rip is fainter than a landing thud, so we need a sensitive trigger.

  5. 5
    Verify with one CMJ

    Coach stands on the tape, dips into a counter-movement, explodes up, lands clean. App listens for two impacts: tape rip → landing thud. Confirms the rip is loud enough before any athlete is tested.

  6. 6
    Run the class

    Athlete steps onto fresh tape, jumps when ready. Phone listens continuously. Tap ✓ to accept, ↺ to redo, or ✎ to override. Auto-advances after 4s. Coach reapplies tape between reps.

Safety
  • 10-min warm-up: jog + dynamic squats + 2 practice half-effort CMJs
  • Athletes land softly — bend knees on impact, do not lock out
  • Tape on the front of the shoe only — never on the heel (trip risk on landing)
  • Stop on knee or ankle pain — refer for assessment
  • Brief athletes: jump straight up, not forward — drift will throw the tape rip timing
