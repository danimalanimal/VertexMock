Counter-Movement Jump Coming soon

Pure-CMJ flight time measured by your phone's camera — no plyo box, no equipment.

Why a separate test?

Drop Jump measures reactive strength using two solid floor impacts. CMJ is the gold-standard test for concentric power — squat down, explode up, no run-up, no box. The trouble is that take-off from a CMJ produces no real impact sound: the feet leave the floor silently. To time flight accurately we need vision, not audio.

How it'll work

  1. 1
    Phone on tripod

    Camera-side facing the athlete from 3-4m away, framed waist-down.

  2. 2
    One-tap calibration

    Athlete stands still in frame; app locks onto floor line + foot position.

  3. 3
    Counter-movement jump

    Athlete jumps. Camera tracks feet leaving the floor and returning.

  4. 4
    Flight time → height

    Same physics as Drop Jump: h = g·t²/8. No RSI (no contact phase).

  5. 5
    Manual fallback

    Tape on the wall, chalk on fingers — same numpad entry as every other Vertex test.

Status

Build pending. Manual entry will ship first; vision-based detection follows once we validate the on-device performance budget on iPhone 13 / Android mid-range.

Use Drop Jump in the meantime for explosive lower-body assessment — same units, same roster, same results pipeline.
