Protocol

T-Test — 4-cone T course. Forward sprint, lateral shuffle, backpedal.

Course

10 yd forward → 5 yd left shuffle → 10 yd right shuffle → 5 yd left shuffle → 10 yd backpedal.

How to run

    Safety

    • Non-slip surface, all cones secured, lane clear of bags/equipment
    • Refuse re-attempts on tweaks — note + refer for assessment
