VERTEX Beep Test

How to run the beep test

  1. 1
    Set up the course

    Two parallel lines exactly 20 m apart. Mark with cones, tape, or chalk. Indoor surface preferred (gym, hall). Allow 1 m clearance behind each line for turning.

  2. 2
    Audio & positioning

    Place phone or speaker mid-court. Volume must reach the furthest runner clearly. Coach stands at the finish line to spot late arrivals. Athletes line up behind line A, single-file with at least 1 m spacing.

  3. 3
    How the test works

    On each beep, athlete must have at least one foot across the opposite line. They turn and wait if early. Two consecutive missed beeps = out. Speed increases every minute (a new "level"). Encourage athletes to push to true exhaustion.

  4. 4
    Eliminating runners

    When a runner can't keep up, tap their tile. The app records their final level + shuttle and adds them to the "out" strip. The toast lets you undo within 2 seconds if they were dropped by mistake.

  5. 5
    After the test

    Walking cool-down (3–5 min), water available. Tap Save to fitness on the results screen — each athlete's beep stat updates. Athletes log subjective effort (RPE) in the Coach Input page.

Safety
  • No fasted athletes — small carb snack 60–90 min prior
  • Asthma inhalers within reach
  • Stop immediately on chest pain, dizziness, or loss of coordination
  • 10-minute warm-up first (light jog, dynamic stretches, 2× practice shuttles)
