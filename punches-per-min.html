VERTEX Punches / Min

Duration

60s — standard Punches Per Minute test, used in boxing combine.

How to run

  1. 1
    Set-up

    Athlete on the heavy bag or pads. Coach holds the phone facing the athlete. Brief them on the count rule: only clean, full-extension punches count.

  2. 2
    Cue + start

    "Ready, GO." Tap START — 3s countdown then "GO." Audio cue confirms.

  3. 3
    Count

    Tap the big pad once per punch. The pad covers most of the screen so you can keep your eyes on the athlete and tap with your thumb.

  4. 4
    Cues during

    Audio beeps at the halfway mark + a 10s countdown at the end. Don't pace them — they should go all-out.

  5. 5
    Scoring

    Final count + punches/minute extrapolation + 10s split. Save to fitness stats so they appear on athlete progress reports.

Safety
  • Wrap hands properly + warm-up shoulders (band pull-aparts, arm circles)
  • Stop on shoulder/wrist pain — don't push through
  • 5-min cool-down jog or shadow box after
