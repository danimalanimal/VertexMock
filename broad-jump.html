VERTEX Broad Jump

Units

Standing broad jump — explosive lower-body power. 3 attempts each, best of 3.

How to run the standing broad jump

  1. 1
    Course

    Flat non-slip surface. Mark a take-off line with tape. Lay a measuring tape perpendicular for 3m+. Soft mat optional for landing.

  2. 2
    Start position

    Feet shoulder-width, toes just behind line. Arms swing freely. No run-up, no foot lifting before jump.

  3. 3
    Jump

    Athlete swings arms, bends knees, jumps forward, lands on both feet. Must stick the landing — no falling backward, no extra step.

  4. 4
    Measure

    Measure from take-off line to back of the heel closest to take-off. Tap the athlete tile → number pad → enter distance.

  5. 5
    Repeat

    3 attempts each, 30-60s rest between. Best of 3 scored. If they fall back or step, mark as foul (0) and retry.

Safety
  • 10-min warm-up: jog + dynamic squats + 2 practice half-effort jumps
  • Confirm landing zone is clear and non-slip
  • Stop if knee/ankle pain — refer for assessment
