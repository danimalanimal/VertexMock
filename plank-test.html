How to run a group plank

  1. 1
    Set-up

    Mats spaced so you can walk between athletes. Elbows under shoulders, forearms parallel, hands relaxed (not clasped). Feet hip-width.

  2. 2
    Form

    Straight line from ears → shoulders → hips → heels. No sag, no pike. Neutral neck (eyes down, not forward). Glutes engaged. Breathe normally.

  3. 3
    Cue + start

    "3, 2, 1, hold." Start the timer on "hold". Milestone beeps fire at 30s, 1:00, 1:30, 2:00, 3:00 so athletes know their splits without watching.

  4. 4
    Form breaks

    One verbal warning. If they can't correct within 3s, tap their tile to record the time and drop them. Be strict — partial planks aren't useful data.

  5. 5
    Scoring

    Score is hold time in seconds. Age-banded benchmarks shown on the results screen. Excellent (12-14 yrs): 2:00+. Excellent (15-17): 2:30+.

Safety
  • Stop on lower-back pain or shoulder pinch
  • No competing through trembling — that's the end
  • Hydrate after; light cool-down stretch (cat-cow + child's pose)
